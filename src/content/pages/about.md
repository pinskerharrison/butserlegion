## History you can get close to

The Butser IX Legion is a Roman reenactment group based at Butser Ancient Farm in Hampshire. We help visitors explore Roman life through demonstrations, conversation and hands-on experiences.

Our activities have included Roman cooking, learning about school in the Roman world, trying on armour and practising army training with commands in Latin. Activities vary from one event to another.

## At home in a Roman villa

Butser Ancient Farm's reconstructed villa is based on the excavated villa at Sparsholt. The building includes a working reconstruction of Roman central heating, offering a setting in which to explore how people lived.

We also visit museums and heritage events. Check our events programme to find where you can meet us next.
