## Roman life at Butser

We recreate aspects of everyday life in Roman Britain, from cooking and crafts to armour and army training.

At our demonstrations you can talk to members, look at their equipment and find out how it was used.

Our home is the reconstructed Roman villa at Butser Ancient Farm, modelled on the villa excavated at Sparsholt. Check the events page to see when we will be there.
