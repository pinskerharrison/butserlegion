## More than an army. A way of life.

What did Roman food taste like? How did their clothes feel? What was it like to learn, work and live in Roman Britain?

We explore the everyday world behind the armour. From cooking and crafts to military training, the Legion brings history within reach for you, your family and your friends.

Our home is the reconstructed Roman villa at Butser Ancient Farm, modelled on the villa excavated at Sparsholt. It's a remarkable setting for discovering the past together.
