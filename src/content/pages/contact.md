## Visiting the Legion

Our base is Butser Ancient Farm, near Chalton in Hampshire. The Legion is not at the farm every day, so check our events programme before making a special trip to see us.

For farm opening hours, admission and accessibility information, visit the farm's own website. Contact the Legion directly for membership and demonstration enquiries.
