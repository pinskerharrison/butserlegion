## Learning together

Feedback recorded from a school visit to West Berkshire Museum in October 2020 described pupils enjoying the Roman soldier room, kitchen objects, archaeological finds and making catapults.

Other visitors remembered trying on helmets and shields, marching with the soldiers and exploring the Roman villa. These are historical reflections, rather than a promise of activities at every event.
